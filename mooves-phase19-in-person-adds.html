Phase 19 — Full
Open question 2 settled, by constraint: there is no web API for screen brightness, so a mockup cannot promise one. What is available is the Screen Wake Lock API (Safari 16.4+, Chrome), so the build holds a wake lock while the QR is on screen and releases it on Done, on navigating away, or on tab blur. No copy tells the user this, it just doesn't sleep mid-scan. Dark modules on a white card scan fine at normal brightness.

Open question 3 settled: copy is state-based throughout, with no coined noun anywhere. "Add friends" (entry) → "Add everyone here" (action) → "Adding everyone here" (live) → "4 joined" (count) → "Done" (close) → "You added 4 people." (outcome).

Spec correction found while building: the approved spec says the entry mirrors "the existing 'New' button on the Groups sub-tab header". That's stale, the create-group control moved to a sticky bottom bar on 2026-07-22, and the Friends sub-tab already has its own sticky bar holding "Invite friends". The mockup follows the shipped pattern instead, and folds the old share action into the hub rather than leaving two competing buttons. Confirm at approval and I'll amend the spec.

No green anywhere in this phase. Green is reserved for availability, and nothing here is availability, so the success and confirmation states are purple. The only green pixels are the cow's nose dot and the wordmark dot, both shipped brand assets.
