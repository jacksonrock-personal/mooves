The rebalance is cheaper than it looks — measured on the live app

Feed's grow-[2] — the double slot it took when Discover was cut in 24.6 — splits back into two singles. Total grow is 5.32 either way, so "Plan a Moove" keeps the exact width R15 tuned for it and People / Settings do not move a pixel. The only slot that changes is Feed's, which halves. Icons went 30 → 26px and labels 10.5 → 10px to pay for it.

Flip the knob to Today to see what it replaces: the same bar with Feed spread across a double slot and a hole where Discover used to be.

The back arrow is gone — decided

A tab with a back chevron is a tab pretending to be a pushed screen, and no other tab in the app has one. "Near you" now starts at the left edge like every other tab title, and the zip chip keeps the right.

Arrivals from "See all" are not stranded. They leave the way everyone else does, by pressing Feed — which is now sitting two slots to the left of where their thumb already is. "See all" is still a push, not a tab switch, so the phone's own back gesture and the browser back button both still return them to the feed. Nothing about that pill changed.

What has not changed, so nobody has to wonder

24.6's lesson still holds. The tab is not a bet that people will remember to visit the revenue surface — that bet is what failed. Community and Sponsored Mooves still live in the feed, where they get seen; this screen is still the thing a feed genuinely cannot do, which is search and filter. The tab just means someone who is looking for browse has somewhere obvious to press.

"Make it a Moove" still hands off to the feed with ?anchor=, the path 13.8 built for arrivals from outside the feed. "I'd go" still lives in the detail sheet, because the card carries one CTA and one CTA only.
