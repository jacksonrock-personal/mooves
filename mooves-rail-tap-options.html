Option A — text-only

  • One tap from feed to Messages
  • One kind of card on the whole screen
  • Greens = availability, Mooves = commitment. Genuinely different objects
  • Deletes: green joins, who's-in on greens, the 2+ green blast, joiners on your own move card, and the join-while-green prompt (20.5)
  • Green waves are unaffected — they detect concurrency, not joins

Option B — keep the card

  • Two taps to reach Messages
  • Nothing shipped gets deleted
  • Keeps "I'm in" and the roster on greens
  • Costs: the card is now name + a chip the rail already showed + a button + a roster. It repeats the rail more than it adds to it
  • Second tap is discoverable but still a tap between you and the person
The question underneath both: do people actually join a green, or do they just text? Joining made obvious sense when a green was the only object in the app and the 2+ gate produced the group blast. Now that Mooves exist and carry their own joins and their own blast, a green is the one thing that has no plan to commit to — you are committing to "someone is around". Prod currently has 5 green joins across all users, which is real but light.

Worth noting: Option A does not remove joining from the app, it removes it from greens. Every Moove still has "I'm in", a roster and the 2+ group text.
