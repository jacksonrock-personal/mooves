Screen 4 — Push

What changed in this pass

1. They are comments. Renamed everywhere, including the push.

2. One arrow, one section. The separate comments disclosure is gone. The Phase 20 who's-in row is now the only control: it opens to the exact people in, then the comments under them, then the field. Collapsed, a card looks exactly as it does today.

3. No intro copy. The empty state is the field alone, placeholder "Add a comment".

4. Ellipsis on comments. Visible "···" rather than a long press. On your own comment it opens Edit and Delete. On a Moove you host, every comment has one and yours offers Remove.

5. Push says comments.

One consequence of the merge, worth a look before you approve

Comments now live behind the same arrow as the roster, so a joiner cannot tell there are comments without opening the row. The old design leaked that with a "4 comments" label. This is stricter and simpler, and it removes the last count from the card, but it does mean a comment can sit unseen until someone expands, and the push is doing more of the work of telling people something was said. Reasonable given comments only last as long as the Moove. Flagging it rather than burying it.

Still deliberately absent

No speech-bubble icon anywhere, because a speech bubble is the universal sign for chat and this is not that. No reply arrows, no mentions, no reactions on comments, no typing indicator, no read receipts, no live updating, no dot on the Feed tab, and nothing at all on a card you have not joined.
