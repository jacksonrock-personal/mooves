Notes on the build

Why nothing is dimmedBecause the light swaps away the moment you go green, it only ever has to depict one state, so all three lamps can stay lit. That is what makes it recognisable at 54px: a dimmed signal at this size just reads as a dark blob. The tile's own availability is carried where it is carried for everyone else on the rail, by the ring.

ColoursRed #FF3B30 and amber #FFB300 are new, and they are icon colours rather than status tokens, so they do not need a slot in the design system. The green stays green-500 on purpose: it is the same green as the rings two tiles away, and a brighter one there would read as a different kind of green.

One thing to watchThe disc is purple-100, which is also the colour of the date block on PlanCard and the group chips. On a feed with plans visible, the rail now has a purple-100 object at the far left and more purple-100 further down. It looks fine here, but it is worth a glance on a busy feed before this ships.

Copy fallout"Go green" on the tile means "Go free" should retire everywhere, since the two names for one action is what made the old tile ambiguous in the first place. That is a small sweep across the rail, the go-green sheet and the landing page.
