What changed in v2, and the two things still open

AppliedReal CowMark (copied verbatim from the component, so it cannot drift from the app). Sentence case throughout. Plus Jakarta Sans everywhere. Sponsor Portal in the top right, quieter than Open app so the visitor-facing CTA still wins. "Talk to us" is now a mailto to business@makemooves.app. Small print section, sign-off line and founder photo all removed. Every em dash on the page is gone, replaced with commas, colons or a new sentence.

HeadlineThe first one was Outernet's move, not ours. All three options now come from the same place your maker line does: the problem is not that there is nothing to do, it is that nobody knows you are free. "Be easy to invite" is the default because it names a thing you can be rather than a thing you should do, and because it is the only one of the three that still makes sense to somebody with zero friends on the app yet.

Open 1: the sponsor claimRemoving the small print took out the line that said paid listings are labelled, sponsors get counts and never names, and only your own friends see what you are up for. That is fine as long as we do not claim the opposite anywhere, which we now do not. Worth knowing why it was there: an earlier version of this page promised sponsored moves never appear in a friend's feed, which Phase 24 made untrue, and that paragraph was the fix. The claims themselves are still honoured in the product and in the Privacy page. If a sponsor or an app store review ever asks where the disclosure lives, the answer is Privacy, not the landing page.

Open 2: "get your friends in the app asap"It is the right sentiment and it reads as us talking, which is the tone we want. It is also the only imperative on the page aimed at somebody who has not signed up yet, so it asks for work before they have anything. Worth a look in context to decide whether it lands as honest or as pushy. It is one line to soften if you want it.
