Screen 4 — Cancel a Moove
The structural change. Greens are out of the feed entirely. The rail is people, the feed is Mooves — one surface per object, so nothing is interleaved and the striping problem cannot come back. This reverses the earlier "rail = now only" rule, which only existed while greens still needed somewhere to sit in the feed; now that they don't, "who's free" versus "what's happening" is the better split. Rail avatars carry their own time label (Now · This wk · Wknd), and later greens get a softer ring so the eye still finds "right now" first.

A Moove has a day, a green does not. That is the whole formality question: date required, time and place and notes all optional. "Sunday, long walk, lake path" is a legitimate Moove.

Riding along: #1 expandable who's-in (before you decide, not after), #6a "Free until" on your own card (never on the swipe), and bounded reactions. Not here: real comments (its own phase, with the no-in-app-messaging rule consciously amended) and scheduled availability (#3/#4/#7, Phase 21, gated on storing user timezones).

Green still only ever means availability — rail rings, green cards, the swipe. Mooves are white with purple tiles.
