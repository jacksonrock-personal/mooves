What is actually new, component by component

PlanCard gains one chip and nothing else. The chip row already holds the author pill and GroupLabel; “through Marcus” is drawn in GroupLabel’s shape — outlined white, 11px semibold ink-500, the same 11px people glyph — so it reads as a sibling of a label that already lives there rather than a new ornament. The 46px purple tile, the title, the when line, the join pill and the avatar-stack footer are untouched.

PlanComposer gains one toggle card, in VisibilityRow, directly under the audience chips. It is the exact shape 18.2’s “Show who this is shared with” toggle already uses: 1.5px hairline, purple-50 fill, 13px title, 11.5px sub, 44×24 switch that goes green-700 when on. State 3 shows both toggles at once, which is the real crowding risk and the reason the new one sits second.

The join sheet is the MooveActionsSheet confirm variant, unchanged in shape, with the disclosure block added. Hide is that file’s other shape — the native action-sheet list with a “Never mind” row — which currently only your own Moove gets; R29 gives a one-hop-out card the same “···” affordance. Settings reuses the “Suggest me for things” card exactly, including its 46×28 green-500 switch, which is a different switch from the composer’s. Both are shipped; they are drawn as they are, not reconciled.

What the drawing exposed

1. Two toggles stack when a group is picked. State 3 is the honest version: “Show who this is shared with” and “Open to friends of friends” sit one above the other, one of them dead. It is survivable because the dead one carries a reason, but if it reads badly the fix is to hide the FoF card while a group is picked rather than disable it — at the cost of a control that vanishes without explaining itself.

2. The card no longer says “Open to friends of friends”. The first draft had it as a subtitle; against the real card there is nowhere for it to go that is not the when line, and the chip already carries it. Dropped.

3. The “···” button is currently mine-only. On someone else’s card that slot holds the join pill, so Hide cannot go there without moving the primary action. Drawn as a long-press instead — but a long-press with no affordance is a feature nobody finds, and this one is the pressure valve. Worth a real answer.

4. “through Marcus” vs “friend of Marcus”. Drawn with the first: shorter, and it reads as a route rather than as a claim about Marcus.
