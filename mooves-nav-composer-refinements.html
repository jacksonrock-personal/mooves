The one that needs a real decision: iOS ignores placeholder on date and time inputs

<input type="date" placeholder="7/29"> does nothing on iOS Safari, which is exactly why your screenshot showed two blank boxes — that is the browser, not a missed attribute. Three ways out, and I would take the third: (a) ship a styled overlay showing the hint until a value exists, (b) keep them as text inputs and parse, which breaks the native wheel picker people expect, or (c) render them as buttons that read "7/29" and "10:30 AM" in grey and open the native picker on tap. (c) keeps the iOS picker, gives full control of the resting state, and is the pattern the rest of this app already uses for date-shaped choices.

The chips have to start unselected, and that is a behaviour change

Today the composer preselects week (or weekend late in the week), so "When" is already satisfied the moment the sheet opens. Under step gating that would make step 2 flash past — you would type a title and the whole rest of the form would appear at once, which is the wall again. So a coarse Moove now starts with no time chosen. Worth knowing it slightly raises the floor: posting goes from one required field to two.

The cow costs contrast, and it is worth knowing what you are buying

A "+" glyph is the most legible thing that can sit in a 52px circle: one stroke, no detail, readable at a glance and in peripheral vision. The cow is eight overlapping shapes and at 34px the ears and horns start to merge. What you get back is a button that is unmistakably this app's and a mark that repeats between the header and the bar. The badge is what keeps the meaning: the cow says whose button it is, the "+" says what it does. Neither carries it alone, which is why option C fails — remove the badge and the only "add" signal is 4px wide.

What I did not do

The centre button never takes the active-tab colour, because it opens a sheet rather than navigating — a nav item that lights up and then leaves you on the same page is a small lie. Editing an existing Moove skips the gating entirely and shows every field filled, since progressive disclosure is for a blank form and would be obstruction on a full one. And there is no progress dots, no "step 1 of 3" chrome, and no back button between steps: earlier steps stay on screen and editable, so it is one form that grows, not a wizard.
